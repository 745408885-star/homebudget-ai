# Product scope

## Product statement

HomeBudget AI is a budget-planning aid for home-procurement decisions in
mainland China. It helps a user organize priorities and understand possible
spending ranges.

It is not:

- a contractor quotation;
- a product sales quotation;
- an engineering design or equipment-selection service;
- financial advice;
- a substitute for site measurement or professional installation review.

## Current product lines

### V2 mainline

The V2 Alpha experience uses five initial inputs and independent category
modules for furniture, standalone appliances, soft furnishings, storage, and
simple smart-home products.

Budget Engine V2 is not connected. V2 prices are prototype estimates.

### Legacy V1

V1 is a frozen renovation-budget experiment retained for compatibility and
regression testing. New product work should not expand its hard-finish scope.

## Included in V2

- movable furniture;
- standalone appliances;
- curtains, finished lighting, bedding, rugs, cushions, art, and plants;
- move-in storage supplies;
- networking and smart devices requiring no or limited installation.

## Excluded from V2

- demolition and hard-finish construction;
- plumbing, electrical work, waterproofing, wall and ceiling work;
- flooring installation;
- whole-home custom cabinetry;
- central HVAC, underfloor heating, and fresh-air systems;
- appliances requiring cabinet or utility reconstruction;
- contractor labor quotations.
